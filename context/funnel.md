# Business Funnel

> How Rose and Alice Creative converts attention into revenue.
> Created during Daily Brief setup. Read by /prime and Daily Brief.

## Currency
NZD

## Stages

### 1. Website Traffic
People discovering the business online.
- Sessions → ga4_daily.sessions
- New users → ga4_daily.new_users
- Engagement rate → ga4_daily.engagement_rate

### 2. Leads & Pipeline
Prospects who have expressed interest or received a proposal.
- Leads in pipeline → client_snapshot.total_leads

### 3. Active Clients
Paying clients currently being served.
- Active clients → client_snapshot.total_clients
- Reports sent → client_snapshot.report_sent_count
- Invoiced → client_snapshot.invoiced_count

### 4. Revenue
Money collected and outstanding.
- Revenue MTD → xero_daily.revenue_mtd
- Revenue last month → xero_daily.revenue_last_month
- Outstanding invoices → xero_daily.outstanding_amount

### 5. Email Audience
Warm audience being nurtured via email.
- Subscribers → mailchimp_snapshot.member_count
- Open rate → mailchimp_snapshot.open_rate
- Click rate → mailchimp_snapshot.click_rate

## Monthly Targets
- Revenue: NZD 20,000
- Active clients: 20+
- Leads in pipeline: 10+
